# Design Rules

## Border Radius
- Do NOT keep any corners rounded.
- Everything in the user interface MUST have strict zero border radius (`border-radius: 0px`).
- Applies to all buttons, cards, modals, dropdowns, inputs, badges, avatars, and containers.

## Color Palette & Typography
- Strict monochrome white & dark palette.
- All text, icons, progress bars, range sliders, badges, and primary accents MUST use pure white (`#ffffff` / shades of white/light gray on dark surfaces).
- Do not use vibrant colors (no red, green, amber, purple, blue accents).
